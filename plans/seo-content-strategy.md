## Workflow per article

1. **Keyword confirmation** — verify volume/competition in GKP or via SERP analysis.
2. **Competitor structure** — extract H2/H3 headings from top 3 ranking articles.
3. **PAA extraction** — pull "People also ask" questions from Google SERP.
4. **Outline** — combine competitor heading structures + PAA into an ETR-specific outline.
5. **Write** — follow CONTENT_QUALITY_CHECKLIST voice, answer-first, real ETR proof.
6. **Quality gate** — Flesch ≥ 60, no invented facts, trust signals where relevant, CTA.
7. **Add to `lib/blog.ts`** — same data-driven pattern as existing posts.
8. **Build check** — confirm green build, correct route count.

## Constraints (from CLAUDE.md / SEO_CONTENT_GUIDE.md)

- No copied competitor content (D-05: structure as reference only, not copy)
- No invented facts, reviews, project details, or prices not in source docs
- Every new topic must pass the high-intent keyword gate (5 questions)
- Content must follow CONTENT_QUALITY_CHECKLIST voice
- Real ETR proof (testimonials from Customer Reviews.md, trust signals, case studies)
- Internal links to relevant service, suburb, and existing blog pages
- CTA to free on-site measure

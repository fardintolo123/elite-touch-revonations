# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Sydney homeowners planning a bathroom renovation. They are comparing a small number of
high-value contractors and need enough visual proof, process clarity, trust signals, and
contact access to decide whether to request an on-site measure.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Elite Touch Renovations as a family-run Sydney bathroom renovation business,
shows credible examples of its work, explains the renovation services and package starting
points, and turns qualified interest into a phone call or enquiry.

Success means a visitor quickly understands what Elite Touch does, where it works, what the
renovation experience is likely to be like, and what to do next.

## Brand Personality

Careful, warm, and accountable. The interface should feel like a capable family business that
communicates clearly, takes pride in finished work, and treats a customer's home with respect.

## Anti-references

- Generic renovation lead-generation pages that rely on stock photography or unsupported claims.
- Glossy property-showroom pages that hide scope, process, pricing context, or contact details.
- Repeated template cards, decorative image treatments, and visual effects that make real project
  photography harder to trust or understand.

## Design Principles

- Show the real work and identify it honestly; imagery is evidence, not decoration.
- Make the renovation process and next step easy to understand.
- Use visual confidence without sacrificing readable copy, accessible contrast, or mobile clarity.
- Keep the family-run, focused-business character visible without inventing scale or credentials.
- Let the content hierarchy support a considered, high-value purchase rather than create urgency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA practices already established in `DESIGN.md`: readable contrast, visible
keyboard focus, semantic structure, descriptive image alternative text, usable tap targets, and
reduced-motion support. Full-bleed imagery must preserve text legibility and must not be the only
way a page communicates meaning.

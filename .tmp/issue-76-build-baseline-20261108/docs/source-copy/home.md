What actually stops people from renovating 
It is rarely the design. It is almost everything around the design as the quote that drifts, the 
trades who do not show up, the budget that grows mid-build, the disruption nobody warned 
you about. Before we talk about what we do well, here is how we handle the things most 
homeowners are right to worry about. 
"I keep getting quotes that are not really quotes" 
Every Elite Touch project starts with a free on-site measure and a written, fixed-scope quote. 
Inclusions, exclusions, and the allowances for tiles, taps and joinery are spelled out in plain 
language. The quote is the contract — not a starting figure that grows the closer we get to 
your bathroom. 
 
"I am worried about what they will find behind the wall" 
Older Sydney homes hide rot, non-compliant plumbing, and sometimes asbestos. We allow 
a sensible contingency in every quote, inspect substrates as soon as the room is open, and 
tell you what we find before any extra work begins. If something needs to change, you get a 
written variation. There is no "while we were in there" invoice at the end. 
 
"I do not trust 'family business' claims" 
Fair enough. Elite Touch is run by members of the Dawood family — Omar, Farah and Mo. 
You will meet at least one of us at the on-site measure, and the same project manager will 
be your point of contact from quote to handover. The fuller story is on our [ link: About 
page]. 
 
"I will be a small job to someone who would rather do extensions" 
Bathrooms are not a sideline for us. They are the only work we do — [: link: full bathroom 
renovations], [: link: ensuite renovations], [: link: powder room renovations], and [: 
link: combined bathroom-and-laundry renovations].  
 
That focus is why the standard of finish stays consistent across every project. 
 
"The waterproofing terrifies me" 
It should. A failed waterproofing membrane is the single most expensive thing that can go 
wrong in a bathroom — mould inside cavities, rotted framing, damaged ceilings on the floor 
below. Every Elite Touch bathroom is waterproofed to AS 3740-2021 by a certified applicator, 
with a Certificate of Compliance issued on completion. The plumbing is certified separately; 
the electrical is certified separately. You get all three at handover.

"We have nowhere else to wash if it runs long" 
We tell you up front which week is the most disruptive — usually the strip-out and 
waterproofing week — and we plan around it with you, not in spite of you. Households with a 
single bathroom can plan for a temporary arrangement during that specific window rather 
than the whole build. 
 
"I have seen too many renovations that look like the brochure but feel wrong 
to live in" 
Our designer, Farah Dawood, leads the design conversation with the question that matters 
most: how is the room actually going to be used? Storage that fits your routine, lighting that 
flatters in the morning, finishes that handle real life. The look comes after the brief is right. [:

What we build 
We focus on bathrooms. That focus is deliberate. Bathrooms are some of the most 
technically demanding rooms in any home — waterproofing, electrical zoning, plumbing, 
tiling and joinery all converge in a small footprint — and doing them well requires the kind of 
trade discipline that gets diluted when a single crew is also taking on kitchens, decks and 
extensions in the same week. 
A typical Elite Touch project is one of the following: 
•​

•​
A heritage-sensitive bathroom build 
•​
A premium bathroom in natural stone, marble, or large-format porcelain 
•​
A well-finished bathroom refresh delivered within one of our fixed packages 
How we work 
Every Elite Touch project follows the same four stages. 
1. Free on-site consultation and quote 
We visit your home, take measurements, talk through what you're hoping for, and provide a 
fixed-scope written quote — not an estimate that drifts. Inclusions, exclusions and 
assumptions are set out clearly, so there are no surprises later. 
2. Design and product selection 
Farah works with you on layout and finishes. You can source products yourself or use our 
trusted supplier network for vanities, tiles, fixtures and accessories. Either way, you choose 
the finishes; we tell you the implications for the build before you commit. 
3. Build and progress payments 
Once contracted, we book a start date and work to a progressive payment schedule. You are 
never asked for money ahead of agreed milestones. Your project manager stays in contact 
throughout, and all wet-area trades work to the current Australian Standards — including AS 
3740-2021 (waterproofing), AS/NZS 3500 (plumbing), AS/NZS 3000 (electrical wiring rules), 
AS 1288 and AS/NZS 2208 (safety glass), and AS 4586 (slip resistance). 
4. Final inspection and handover 
Before the final payment, we walk through the finished bathroom with you and resolve any 
defects on the spot. You receive your waterproofing certificate of compliance, plumbing and 
electrical certificates of compliance, and the warranty documentation for fixtures and joinery. 
Credentials and protections 
Bathroom renovation is regulated work. Every job is delivered under the licences, insurances 
and statutory protections required by NSW law and the National Construction Code.

Why this level of detail matters 
A bathroom renovation is one of the most regulated rooms in a home and one of the more 
expensive renovations a household typically commits to.  
 
Cutting corners on waterproofing or wet-area electrical work creates problems that stay 
invisible until they are very expensive like leaks behind walls, mould inside cavities, failed 
insurance claims, electric-shock risk in wet zones. 
 
 We do this work for a living, so the standards work is not a separate task at the end of the 
job, it is how every job is built from day one.

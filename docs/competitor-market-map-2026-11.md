**Internal research only. Do not publish this file or use it as public brand-vs-brand comparison copy.**

**Compiled:** 2026-11-10. **Business pages and licence register checked:** 2026-11-09. **Google search captures:** 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10. **Sample:** eight businesses. **Purpose:** source-backed facts for the internal market read and the `/ai/` fit wording.

## Method and limits

- Candidates were taken from organic page-one results and separately identified local-pack results for the Sydney and Tier-1 suburb queries listed below. The sample excludes broad builders without a bathroom-renovation specialty, ads, directories and lead-generation sites.
- Google was opened with `gl=au`, `hl=en`, and `pws=0` on desktop. Google showed the country as Australia; its footer said it could not determine the search location. The broad query showed a Sydney NSW local pack. No Sydney device location is claimed.
- Company facts below are claims displayed on the cited company pages. Licence status, holder, class and conditions are checked against the NSW public register detail API. No other claim is independently audited.
- `Unknown` means the cited page did not state that field. Prices are transcribed with their stated basis; they are not normalized across businesses. Rating figures are recorded only where the cited page displayed a Google-specific figure or claim. Combined review counts are not treated as Google-only counts.
- Every non-Unknown cell has its supporting URL and check date. Sources were checked 2026-11-09 unless the SERP table states otherwise.

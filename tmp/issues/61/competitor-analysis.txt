What the Reviews Reveal: Common Questions, Pain Points &
Objections
● Fixed timelines vs open-ended renovations — an overwhelming theme across Luke's Renovations reviews is jobs
"completed on budget and in just two weeks as promised." The implied objection: fear that a bathroom reno will
drag on for months, as renovations are notorious for doing.
● Waterproofing certification — a public Facebook complaint about a competitor ("no waterproofing certificate or
warranty") shows this is a genuine, high-stakes objection. Buyers are increasingly aware that uncertified
waterproofing can cause leaks, disputes, and resale problems.
● Fixed written quotes vs vague verbal estimates — reviewers repeatedly credit renovators for "a clear process" and
detailed quoting up front, implying customers have been burned by scope creep or surprise costs elsewhere.
● First-time renovator anxiety — several reviews specifically thank the team for "taking the time to answer all our
questions as first time renovators," showing many customers feel out of their depth and want hand-holding.
● One team coordinating all trades — praise for renovators who "run a tight ship" coordinating plumbers, tilers,
waterproofers and electricians suggests customers fear having to project-manage multiple tradespeople themselves.
● No hard sell / trustworthy sales process — phrases like "no hard sell, just an honest conversation" appear in multiple
reviews, indicating customers are wary of pushy sales tactics common in the renovation industry.
● Handling surprises found during demolition — reviews mention unexpected challenges (skylights, structural issues)
being managed smoothly, suggesting customers worry about scope and cost blowouts once walls are opened up.
● Strata/apartment approvals — one competitor's marketing highlights "Strata Approval, Slab Scan, Core Holes" as
selling points, showing this is a real pain point for apartment owners unsure how approvals work.
● Value for quality — reviewers value "quality workmanship, clear communication, proper waterproofing, licensed
trades" over the cheapest price, and explicitly warn that "cheap can end" badly — a useful trust angle.

FAQ Section for elitetouchrenovations.au
Q: How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Sydney?
A: Cost depends on layout changes, fixtures and finishes, but most full bathroom renovations range from the low tens of
thousands upward. Book a free on-site measure for a fixed, itemised quote — not a rough verbal guess.
Q: Do you provide a fixed quote or just an estimate?
A: You receive a fixed-scope written quote before work begins, so there are no surprise costs partway through your
renovation.
Q: Is your waterproofing certified?
A: Yes. All waterproofing is completed to AS 3740 standard and backed by a compliance certificate, which protects you
against leaks and is essential if you ever sell the property.
Q: How long will my bathroom renovation take?
A: Most single bathroom renovations are completed within a few weeks under a clear, structured program — we set a
timeline upfront and stick to it rather than letting the job drag on.
Q: Do you handle strata or apartment approvals?
A: Yes, we can assist with strata approval documentation, slab scans and core hole works required for apartment renovations.
Q: Are you licensed and insured?
A: Yes — we hold NSW Builder Licence 475204C and full insurance cover.
Q: What warranty comes with the renovation?
A: We back our work with a 10-year workmanship warranty.
Q: Can I get a free on-site measure and quote?
A: Yes, we offer a free on-site measure across the Sydney metro area with no obligation.
Q: Will one team manage all the trades on my job?
A: Yes — we coordinate plumbing, tiling, waterproofing and electrical work as one managed project, so you don't have to
schedule or supervise separate tradespeople yourself.
Q: What happens if you find an issue once demolition starts?\n\n=== PAGE 3 ===\nA: We inspect thoroughly before quoting to minimise surprises, and if an issue is uncovered during demolition, we discuss it
